Robo Vice — an offline web app that turns your photos into GTA-style cover art with a robot twist: chrome mecha characters in a Vice City neon sunset. Five multi-panel layouts (four-panel grid, hero-and-three, full bleed, tilted snapshots, nine-panel grid), per-panel crop / position / zoom, six gradient-map neon grades, bold Anton title lettering with a Pacifico neon script subtitle, a title block with a hand-drawn sunset skyline, film grain and vignette, undo / redo, and one-step export of 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080 PNGs. Photos never leave your machine. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6 Cover Art” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Pick a layout and a grade, type a title, drag any panel's photo into place (scroll to zoom), and hit **Export all 3 sizes (PNG)**.

Everything is original: the three sample photos are AI-made robot characters (no real person), the lettering is Anton and Pacifico under the SIL Open Font License (vendored in `app/fonts/` with the OFL text), and there is no Rockstar Games logo, artwork, character or font anywhere in the app.
